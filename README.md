# 📊 Global Sales Dynamics & ABC Analysis (Google Sheets)

An end-to-end e-commerce sales analytics project focused on evaluating multi-region sales performance, shipping lead times, and product portfolio efficiency using Google Sheets.

🔗 **[View Interactive Google Sheets Project](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1pBPZ7q-BCP96K2dDnmlvtTZxkJ5sMEQANwIpTrfS9zw/edit?usp=sharing)**

---

## 📌 Project Overview
The objective of this project is to analyze sales data for a global e-commerce company operating across online and offline channels. The analysis covers revenue, profitability, geographic expansion, shipping efficiency, and Pareto-based product prioritization.

---

## 🛠️ Key Analytical Tasks & Methodology

1. **Data Cleaning & Enrichment:**
   * Consolidated datasets (`events`, `products`, `countries`).
   * Cleaned missing values and fixed anomalies.
   * Calculated custom metrics: **Total Revenue**, **Total Cost**, and **Total Profit**.

2. **Core Performance Metrics:**
   * Calculated high-level KPIs: total orders, total revenue, net profit, and geographic coverage (number of countries/regions).

3. **Geographic & Channel Breakdown:**
   * Analyzed performance across countries, regions, and sub-regions.
   * Compared **Online vs. Offline** sales channel performance.

4. **Shipping & Lead Time Analysis:**
   * Calculated order-to-shipment duration across regions and product categories.
   * Evaluated whether delivery speed impacts overall profitability.

5. **Sales Dynamics & Seasonality:**
   * Identified sales trends and seasonality across months, years, and days of the week (`WEEKDAY`).

6. **Pareto (ABC) Product Analysis:**
   * Categorized products into **A, B, and C** tiers based on order volume, revenue, and profit contribution for the latest year.

7. **Executive Dashboard & Documentation:**
   * Built a clean, interactive mini-dashboard in Google Sheets with charts and KPIs.
   * Summarized findings and actionable business recommendations.

---

## 🧰 Tools Used
* **Google Sheets:** Pivot Tables, Advanced Formulas (`QUERY`, `FILTER`, `XLOOKUP`, `WEEKDAY`), Slicers, Data Visualization, Conditional Formatting.
* **Documentation:** Structured Executive Summary & Business Insights report.
